Tathagat Trust is a charitable organization founded in 2010, based in Patparganj Industrial Area, New Delhi, committed to education, skill development, and inclusive growth for India's most marginalized communities.

Explore Our Work Support Our Mission
Our Approach

A Two-Pronged Strategy

Awareness that opens minds, and support that opens doors — working together so communities can walk their own path with dignity.

Creating Awareness

Engaging parents and children toward constructive socio-economic opportunities — so education and skill are chosen as the first path, not the last resort.

Providing Direct Support

Education, technical skills, career counseling, entrepreneurial and employment pathways — practical support that turns intention into livelihood.

What Guides Us

Core Philosophy

ISR + CSR

Individual & Corporate Social Responsibility working together — citizens and companies sharing the duty of nation-building.

Constructive Engagement

Education and vocational skills as the lasting solution to marginalization, crime, and unrest.

Breaking Intergenerational Cycles

Using skills and education as tools for empowerment and dignity — so the next generation inherits opportunity, not stigma.

Funding Integrity

Started with family/friends support, rental income, audited accounts, regular ITR filing.

Dr. N.P. Singh, IAS (Retd.)

Sanrakshak (Patron)

A distinguished administrator, educationist, and Hindi literary figure, Dr. N.P. Singh has guided Tathagat Trust from its founding vision to its work on the ground.

He led the Naini Lake cleaning initiative (2000); founded Tathagat ITI, Bawaria rehabilitation, Musahar scholarships; currently Executive Chairman, Bhartiya Shiksha Board (Govt. of India / Patanjali Yogpeeth).
